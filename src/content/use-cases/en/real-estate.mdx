## Termpack `real-estate`

The first real-estate pack includes: Exposé, Grundbuch, Energieausweis, Wohnfläche, Käufer, Besichtigung, Provision, and Kaufvertrag.

## Recommended Profiles

Start with one profile for email and CRM fields, a short note profile for viewings, and a formatting prompt for listing sections. The pack helps with domain vocabulary recognition, but it does not verify property details.

## License Note

The project can remain open source. Industry-specific term packs are shown and activated in the app only when a commercial license is active.

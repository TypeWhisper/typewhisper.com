## Termpack `architecture`

The first architecture pack includes: HOAI, Leistungsverzeichnis, Ausführungsplanung, Bauleitung, Mängel, Aufmaß, Statik, and Bauherr.

## Recommended Profiles

Useful first profiles are a short site-note profile, an email profile for clients and consultants, and a meeting-minutes prompt for structured site meetings. The pack helps with vocabulary recognition, but it does not perform technical assessment.

## License Note

The industry pack is a commercial app artifact: without a commercial license, the industry choice is stored, but the pack is not shown or activated.

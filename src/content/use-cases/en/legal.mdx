## Termpack `legal`

The first legal pack includes: Mandat, Schriftsatz, Klageschrift, Frist, Vollmacht, Anspruch, DSGVO, and Vergleich.

## Recommended Profiles

For v1, simple profiles are enough: a short case-note profile, an email profile, and a cautious prompt for more readable draft wording. TypeWhisper does not assess legal merits, check deadlines, or replace attorney review.

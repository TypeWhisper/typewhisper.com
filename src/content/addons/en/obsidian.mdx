## One add-on family, two editions

Both editions save completed TypeWhisper transcriptions as regular Markdown files inside a selected Obsidian vault. Setup, available templates, and automation differ between macOS and Windows.

Choose your platform above. Its page contains only the features, requirements, settings, and examples that apply to that edition.

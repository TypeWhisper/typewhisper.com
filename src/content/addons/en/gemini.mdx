## About

Google's Gemini 2.5 models provide excellent LLM capabilities for text processing tasks. Choose between Flash (fast and free), Pro (highest quality), and Flash Lite (minimal latency) depending on your needs. Use Gemini as your LLM provider for custom prompts, text correction, summarization, and more.

## Features

- Gemini 2.5 Flash with generous free tier
- Gemini 2.5 Pro for maximum quality
- Flash Lite for ultra-low latency
- Excellent multilingual support
- Long context window

## LLM Models

| Model | ID |
| --- | --- |
| Gemini 2.5 Flash | `gemini-2.5-flash` |
| Gemini 2.5 Pro | `gemini-2.5-pro` |
| Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite | `gemini-2.5-flash-lite` |

## Configuration

- **API Key** - Get a free API key from Google AI Studio (aistudio.google.com).

## Setup

1. Open TypeWhisper Settings > Plugins
2. Find the Gemini plugin and click Configure
3. Enter your Gemini API key
4. Select Gemini as your LLM provider in a custom prompt

## About

Local LLM (llama.cpp) runs workflows and prompts on your Mac with [llama.cpp](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp) and Metal. Cleanup, rewrites, summaries and translations work without sending text to the cloud. Besides the recommended Gemma 4 models, you can use any GGUF model from Hugging Face, for example a small 0.5B to 4B model that cleans up dictations in about a second, or import a `.gguf` file you already have.

## Features

- Fully local prompt processing with llama.cpp and Metal on Apple silicon
- Gemma 4, Qwen3.5 and LFM2.5 models, the same files as in TypeWhisper for Windows
- Any GGUF model from Hugging Face: enter the repository, pick a file, done
- Import of `.gguf` files from your Mac
- Downloads pinned to the repository's current commit and checked against their SHA-256
- Thinking turned off for text cleanup, so answers come right away
- Generation temperature slider for more precise or more creative output
- Optional Hugging Face token for higher download rate limits and private or gated models
- No API key required

## Recommended Models

| Model | Download Size | Recommended RAM | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gemma 4 E2B (Q4_K_M) | ~3.1 GB | 8 GB+ | Recommended |
| Gemma 4 E4B (Q4_K_M) | ~5 GB | 16 GB+ | Recommended |
| Gemma 4 E4B (Q8_0) | ~8.2 GB | 16 GB+ | Supported |
| Gemma 4 26B-A4B (Q4_K_M, MoE) | ~17 GB | 32 GB+ | Experimental |
| Qwen3.5 2B (Q4_K_M) | ~1.3 GB | 8 GB+ | Experimental |
| LFM2.5 2.6B (Q4_K_M) | ~1.7 GB | 8 GB+ | Experimental |

The plugin recommends Gemma 4 E2B or E4B based on your Mac's memory.

## Your Own Models

- **From Hugging Face** - Enter a repository such as `unsloth/Qwen3.5-2B-GGUF`, add a quantization like `unsloth/Qwen3.5-2B-GGUF:Q4_K_M`, or paste a huggingface.co link. Click `Find GGUF Files`, pick a file and click `Add & Download`.
- **From a file** - Click `Import GGUF File…` and choose a `.gguf` file. The plugin copies it into its own folder; on the Mac's internal disk this takes no extra space, and your original file stays where it is.

Models with a chat template in their GGUF file work best; most instruct and chat models have one. Models split into several files and projector files (`mmproj-*.gguf`) are not offered.

## Setup

1. In TypeWhisper, open `Settings > Discover plugins` and install `Local LLM (llama.cpp)`. The add-on then appears in the Settings sidebar, and its page opens.
2. Pick a recommended model and click `Download & Load`, or add your own model under `Your Models`. Experimental models use `Try anyway`.
3. After the model is loaded, select `Local LLM (llama.cpp)` as the LLM provider in your workflow or prompt.

## Notes

- The first model also downloads llama.cpp 0.7.0 (about 12 MB) from GitHub. It runs as a local server that only your Mac can reach, with a new random key on every start.
- After Auto-unload or a crash of the model server, the next prompt loads the model again.
- The plugin needs a Mac with Apple silicon and TypeWhisper 1.8.0 or later. It is not available in the App Store edition, because it runs the downloaded llama.cpp program.
- LFM2.5 uses the LFM Open License, which allows free commercial use only below USD 10 million in annual revenue. Models you add keep the license of their repository.

## About

SOV-Transcribe runs [Salesforce SOV-Transcribe-1](https://huggingface.co/salesforce-sovereign-intelligence/SOV-Transcribe-1) on your Mac through MLX. The model combines a Whisper large-v3 audio encoder with a Gemma 4 mixture-of-experts decoder and writes transcripts with punctuation and capitalization in English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, and Dutch.

This is a local engine. Your audio is transcribed on your Mac. The add-on uses the network to download the model.

## Requirements

This release requires **TypeWhisper 1.8.3 or newer** on macOS 14 or later and a Mac with **Apple silicon and 32 GB of memory or more**.

The add-on offers the 4-bit model. It is a download of about 15.6 GB and uses about 16 GB of memory while it transcribes. You need no API key and no account.

On a Mac with less than 32 GB of memory the add-on offers no model to TypeWhisper. Its settings show the model with the memory requirement, and **Download & Load** stays unavailable.

## Setup

1. In TypeWhisper, open **Settings > Discover plugins** and install **SOV-Transcribe**. The add-on then appears in the Settings sidebar, and its page opens.
2. Select **Download & Load** beside **SOV-Transcribe-1 (4-bit)**.
3. Select **SOV-Transcribe (MLX)** as your transcription engine in the dictation settings or in a profile.

## Settings

| Setting | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| **HuggingFace Token** | Optional. A token raises the download rate limits of Hugging Face. **Save** checks the token with Hugging Face and stores a valid one in the macOS Keychain. **Remove** deletes the stored token. |
| **Model** | Shows SOV-Transcribe-1 (4-bit) with its size and memory requirement. **Download & Load** downloads the model and loads it; when the files are already on your Mac the button is called **Load**. **Unload** releases the model from memory and keeps the downloaded files. |
| **1 downloaded model** | Expands to the downloaded model. There you delete its files when you no longer need them. |

## Transcription

The add-on shows text while the model writes it. When you choose a language for dictation, the add-on tells the model which language it hears. Without a selected language the model recognizes the language on its own. Translation is not supported.

The add-on passes your TypeWhisper dictionary terms to the model as a contextual vocabulary, up to 4,000 characters. This helps the model spell names and technical terms the way you write them. Dictionary corrections are still applied after transcription.

The model handles up to 30 minutes in one pass. The add-on splits longer recordings into parts of at most 30 minutes at a quiet moment and transcribes them one after the other.

## Local processing and privacy

Recognition runs on your Mac. The add-on sends no audio and no text to a cloud service.

The model files come from the Hugging Face repository [`TypeWhisper/SOV-Transcribe-1-4bit`](https://huggingface.co/TypeWhisper/SOV-Transcribe-1-4bit), a 4-bit MLX conversion of Salesforce's original weights. TypeWhisper stores them in `~/Library/Application Support/TypeWhisper/PluginData/com.typewhisper.sov-transcribe/models`. The add-on also contacts Hugging Face when it checks a token.

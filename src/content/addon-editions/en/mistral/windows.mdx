## Setup

1. In TypeWhisper, open **Settings > Discover plugins** and install **Mistral**. The add-on then appears in the Settings sidebar, and its page opens.
2. Enter your Mistral API key under **API key**, choose **Save settings**, and verify the key with **Check connection**.
3. Choose **Refresh models** to load the models available to your account. Until you refresh, the add-on offers the current lineup listed below.
4. Choose a **Transcription model**. Voxtral Mini Transcribe Realtime 3 shows live text while you speak when **Live transcription** is turned on under **Appearance**; Voxtral Mini Transcribe 3 and Voxtral Small Transcribe 3 transcribe after you stop.
5. For text processing, choose the **Text model**, its **Reasoning effort** if the model has one, and the **Temperature**, then choose **Save settings**.
6. Under **Settings > Dictation**, select **Mistral** as the **Provider**. For text processing, select Mistral as a workflow's **Provider** under **Advanced** or as the **Default LLM**.

## Models

| Transcription model | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Voxtral Mini Transcribe 3 | Default; files, recordings and the final text of live dictations |
| Voxtral Small Transcribe 3 | Mistral's most accurate transcription model, according to Mistral |
| Voxtral Mini Transcribe Realtime 3 | Live text while you dictate (TypeWhisper 1.3 or newer) |

| Text model | Reasoning effort |
| --- | --- |
| Mistral Large 4 | Low (default), Medium, High |
| Mistral Medium 3.5 | Off (default), High |
| Mistral Small 4 (default) | Off (default), High |
| Ministral 3 14B, 8B and 3B | – |

The reasoning effort set for a model also applies when a workflow uses it. With Voxtral Mini Transcribe Realtime 3 selected, the final text of a dictation, files and complete recordings come from Voxtral Mini Transcribe 3. An account without a file transcription model keeps the live text as the final text.

## Prices and privacy

Mistral's list prices in November 2026, per hour of audio: Voxtral Mini Transcribe 3 $0.21, Voxtral Small Transcribe 3 $0.24 and Voxtral Mini Transcribe Realtime 3 $0.48. Dictations with live text are billed for the realtime model and the final upload, about $0.69 per hour. Text models are billed per token. A model in the list is not a promise that your workspace may use it: a workspace limit of zero requests per minute makes requests fail with a rate-limit error.

Audio and text are sent to Mistral for the capability you select. Opening the add-on's page sends no request; only **Check connection** and **Refresh models** contact Mistral.

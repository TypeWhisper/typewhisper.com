## Setup

1. Open **Settings > Integrations** and enable **Gemini**.
2. Save your Google AI Studio API key and refresh the model catalog.
3. Select Gemini as the transcription provider and choose an available transcription model.
4. Choose **Smart** for cleaned dictation or **Verbatim** for a more literal result. Live previews require a model with live support.
5. For text processing, select Gemini separately as the workflow's LLM provider and choose a Gemini or Gemma text model.

## Models and privacy

Transcription and language models are filtered separately. Available models depend on Google's catalog and your API access. The transcription provider does not support translation requests; translation can run as a separate text workflow.

Audio and text are sent to Google for the capability you select. Billing and usage limits depend on your Google account.

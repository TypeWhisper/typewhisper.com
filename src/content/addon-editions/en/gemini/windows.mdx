## Setup

1. In TypeWhisper, open **Settings > Discover plugins** and install **Google Gemini**. The add-on then appears in the Settings sidebar, and its page opens.
2. Enter your Gemini API key from Google AI Studio under **API key**, choose **Save settings**, and verify the key with **Check connection**.
3. Choose **Refresh models** to load the transcription and text models available to your key. Until you refresh, the add-on offers Gemini 3.5 Transcribe and the Gemini Flash Lite, Flash and Pro "latest" text models.
4. Choose a **Transcription model**. Under **Transcription mode**, choose **Smart** for cleaned-up text or **Verbatim** to keep the spoken wording. Live text while recording needs a transcription model that has a live version, such as Gemini 3.5 Transcribe.
5. For text processing, choose the default **Text model**, set **Temperature** to **Provider default** or **Custom**, and if needed enter a value from 0 to 2 under **Custom temperature**. Choose **Save settings**.
6. Under **Settings > Dictation**, select **Google Gemini** as the **Provider**; a **Dictation Only** workflow can also use its own transcription model. For text processing, select Google Gemini as a workflow's **Provider** under **Advanced** or as the **Default LLM**; the workflow can choose a different Gemini or Gemma model.

## Models and privacy

Transcription and text models are filtered into separate catalogs. Which models you can use depends on Google's catalog and your API access. Opening the add-on's page sends no request; only **Refresh models** fetches and saves the catalogs. The transcription provider does not support translation requests; translation can run as a separate text workflow.

Audio and text are sent to Google for the capability you select. Recorded audio is uploaded through Google's Files API and transcribed with a request that sets `store: false`; the uploaded file is deleted on a best-effort basis after the request succeeds, fails, or is canceled. Billing and usage limits depend on your Google account.
